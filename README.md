# Bayesian-LSTM - Modelling of Medical Insurance Cost
Accurate forecasting of medical insurance costs is crucial for insurers, healthcare providers, and policymakers in today’s rapidly changing healthcare environment. Emphasis is placed not only on the accuracy of predictive models but also on their reliability. Traditional machine learning models can effectively capture complex patterns in cost data, but they often fail to account for uncertainty in their predictions, which can lead to unreliable estimates. This study utilized a Bayesian LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory) model to predict medical insurance costs and compared its performance with that of a conventional LSTM model. Results showed that the Bayesian LSTM model outperformed the conventional LSTM based on metrics such as R-squared, MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error), and RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error). By recognizing the inherent uncertainty in medical cost data, the Bayesian LSTM model avoided overconfidence in its predictions, demonstrating a confidence level of 65.32% when forecasting insurance costs in the dataset analyzed. This approach offers a more dependable method for predicting medical insurance costs, enabling stakeholders to make better-informed financial and policy decisions.
